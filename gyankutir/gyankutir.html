We are starting the process of opening a library for children in the village, and we hope for support from all of you. If you have books, or anything else a library can use (chairs, tables, shelves, a fan), lying unused and you would like to donate it, please contact us.

Why a Library?

01

No Reading Room for 15 km

There is no library or reading room in Gopalpura or the villages around it. Students travel 10–15 km just to find a quiet place to sit and study. GyanKutir brings that place to their doorstep.

02

A Safe Space to Learn

Most homes here have neither the space nor the resources for focused study. A quiet, lit, furnished room gives children a place to sit, read, and do homework every single day.

03

Books Beyond Textbooks

Most children in Gopalpura have never owned a storybook. A library puts stories, science, and the wider world within walking distance of every home.

04

GramSutr Lives On

GramSutr was a 3-day spark. GyanKutir is the permanent flame: a lasting institution the village community will run long after the camp ends.

What You Can Donate

Anything a library needs: if it helps a child sit and read, it belongs here.

Books

Storybooks, comics, NCERT textbooks, exam-prep books, dictionaries, atlases: Hindi or English.

Bookshelves & Almirahs

Shelves, racks, or steel almirahs to keep books safe from dust and damp.

Chairs & Mats

Chairs, benches, durries, or floor mats so children can sit comfortably and read.

Tables & Desks

Study tables, desks, or low writing tables for reading and homework.

Cooler / Fan

Ceiling fans, table fans, or a cooler: Bundelkhand summers are brutal; reading should not be.

Stationery & Lights

Notebooks, pens, charts, a whiteboard, emergency lights, or an inverter for power cuts.

Gently used items are welcome. Please make sure books have no missing pages and furniture or appliances are in working condition.
